# simple-interest-calc# Simple Interest Calculator

## 1. Introduction

The Simple Interest Calculator is a basic financial calculation project designed to calculate the simple interest on a given principal amount based on the interest rate and time period.

Simple interest is one of the easiest methods of calculating interest on a loan, investment, or deposited amount. This calculator helps users understand how much interest will be earned or paid over a specified period.

## 2. Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop a simple and user-friendly calculator that calculates the interest amount and total amount using the simple interest formula.

This project also helps beginners understand basic mathematical operations, input handling, and programming logic.

## 3. Simple Interest Formula

The formula used to calculate simple interest is:

**Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100**

Where:

- **P** – Principal Amount: The initial amount of money invested or borrowed.
- **R** – Rate of Interest: The annual interest rate expressed as a percentage.
- **T** – Time Period: The duration for which the money is invested or borrowed, measured in years.
- **SI** – Simple Interest: The additional amount earned or paid as interest.

### Total Amount Formula

**Total Amount = Principal Amount + Simple Interest**

## 4. Working Principle

The calculator works based on the following steps:

1. Get the principal amount from the user.
2. Get the annual rate of interest.
3. Get the time period in years.
4. Multiply the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.
5. Divide the result by 100.
6. Display the calculated simple interest.
7. Add the simple interest to the principal amount.
8. Display the final total amount.

## 5. Example Calculation

Consider the following example:

| Input | Value |
|---|---:|
| Principal Amount | ₹10,000 |
| Rate of Interest | 5% |
| Time Period | 2 Years |

**Step 1: Apply the formula**

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

**Step 2: Substitute the values**

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

**Step 3: Calculate the interest**

SI = 1,00,000 / 100

SI = ₹1,000

**Step 4: Calculate the total amount**

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Total Amount = 10,000 + 1,000

**Final Result:**
- Simple Interest = ₹1,000
- Total Amount = ₹11,000
